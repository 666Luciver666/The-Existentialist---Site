Background
Manuel Cech began performing magic at the age of three and later became a certified pyrotechnician and illusion engineer in Germany. He has worked in stage productions, music videos, and large-scale special effects for over two decades before dedicating himself to fine art and existential realism.

Current Work
The first major artwork of “The Existentialist” Series, Lucia, was completed in 2025. It marks the beginning of a new chapter combining realism, emotion, and philosophical depth.
