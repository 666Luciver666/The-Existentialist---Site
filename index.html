About Manuel Cech

By Manuel Cech
Multidisciplinary Artist · Magician · Musician
Based on Fuerteventura, Canary Islands.

Exploring identity, decay, illusion and transformation through Existential Realism.

Art & Media
— Fine Art Prints (Pastel, Acrylic & Airbrush)
— Digital Artwork · Sculpture & Mask Design
— Animatronics & SFX Creations · Professional Illusion Design

Background
Began performing magic at age three; later a certified pyrotechnician and illusion engineer in Germany. Worked across stage productions, music videos and large-scale special effects before focusing on fine art and existential realism.

Current Work
The first milestone of The Existentialist series, “Lucia”, was completed in 2025 — combining realism, emotion and philosophical depth.
